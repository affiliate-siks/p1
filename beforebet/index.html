Live, weekly

Weekly Quiz

Every Thursday at 20:00, everyone answers the same questions at the same time. Miss it and that round is gone — no replay, no catch-up. That's what keeps it fair.

How it stays fair

●Questions unlock for everyone at the exact same second — no early start, no going back once answered.
●Short per-question timer — long enough to know it, too short to look it up.
●Answer order is shuffled differently for every player, so screen-sharing an answer key doesn't work.
●One verified account per phone number required to play — no duplicate entries stacking the leaderboard.
